I am a Senior Research Analyst at the [AI Economics Institute](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introducing-the-ai-economics-institute), where I lead economic modelling and scenario analysis.

Before joining the Institute, I was a Senior Analyst at the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT). I modelled the potential impacts of AI and analysed evidence on business adoption and AI skills to inform policy in both areas. As part of this work, we published the [AI adoption survey 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-survey-2026). I also helped develop [AI Scenarios 2030](/policy/ai-scenarios-2030/) with colleagues across government, exploring five possible futures for AI.

Previously, I was a Research Associate at the [Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, University of Cambridge](https://www.lcfi.ac.uk/), where I remain an [Associate Fellow](https://www.lcfi.ac.uk/people/marko-tesic-2). My research there covered benchmark validity, the capabilities of large language models, and [how those capabilities match the demands of workplace tasks](/current_projects/workforce_suitability/). This work was supported by the [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/projects/artificial-intelligence-and-future-of-skills.html).

As a [Royal Academy of Engineering UK IC](https://raeng.org.uk/ukicpostdoc) Postdoctoral Research Fellow, I studied how explanations of AI predictions influence people’s beliefs. My research has also covered causal and probabilistic reasoning, data analysis and Bayesian modelling.
