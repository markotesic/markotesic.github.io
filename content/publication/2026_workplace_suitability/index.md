We assess AI suitability for workplace activities by representing both systems and tasks along shared cognitive dimensions. The study profiles six AI systems and gathers task requirements from 410 employees across six occupational domains. These profiles provide a comparative tool for deciding where to pilot AI and where its current capabilities may be insufficient.

This paper reports work from the [AI Workforce Suitability project](/current_projects/workforce_suitability/) and builds on my [original AI Workforce Suitability GitHub repository](https://github.com/markotesic/ai-workforce-suitability).
