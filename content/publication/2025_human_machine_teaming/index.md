This invited review brings together UK and US research on integrating human and AI strengths in teams. It examines brain–computer interfaces, trust, explanations, deception, cognitive models and teamwork, with a focus on defence applications and the challenges of moving from separate human and machine functions to adaptive collaboration.

Published in the 2025 volume of *BMJ Military Health*. The article first appeared online on 24 December 2024.
